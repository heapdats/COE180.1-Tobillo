Design of Binary Adders LAB 2.
o Objectives: What was the goal of this lab?

■ Understand the principles of fundamental combinational arithmetic circuits.
■ Design and implement half adders and full adders in SystemVerilog.
■ Design and implement half subtractors and full subtractors in SystemVerilog.
■ Apply all three modeling styles (structural, dataflow, behavioral) to a practical design.
■ Simulate and verify the functionality of these circuits using a testbench.
■ Analyze the trade-offs between different modeling approaches.
■ Understand the advantages of SystemVerilog over traditional Verilog.


o Design Description: 
We made half adders, full adders, half subtractors, and full subtractor gates using verilog code using Dataflow, Behavioural, and Structural design

o Simulation Results & Waveforms:
The testbench used iterated through all possible inputs for the gates to get all the possible outputs
